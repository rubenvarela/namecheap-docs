# Are there any alternate Dynamic DNS clients?

Yes, there are many clients available on the internet. Some clients that may support Namecheap [Dynamic DNS](article.aspx__33__11__what-is-dynamic-dns.md) are provided below.

Please note that the clients supporting Namecheap DNS may or may not be available immediately. Please contact the respective provider for more information or request adding Namecheap support to their clients.

Mac: [NC DNS Updater](https://github.com/aidanamavi/nc-dns-updater), [IP Monitor](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/ip-monitor/id1050307950?mt=12)
Unix: [DDClient](https://github.com/ddclient/ddclient/releases), [IPCheck](http://zoneclient.sourceforge.net/)
Other: [Java Dynamic DNS Client](http://sourceforge.net/projects/rzodyndns/), [IPCheck for OS/2](http://zoneclient.sourceforge.net/)
